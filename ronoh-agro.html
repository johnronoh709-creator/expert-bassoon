Meet Ronoh — Your Trusted Agronomist

I am Ronoh, a professional agronomist based in Nakuru City, Kenya, holding a BSc. in Agriculture and Enterprise Development. With hands-on expertise spanning all stages of crop production, I help farmers at every level unlock their land's true potential.

Whether you manage a quarter-acre kitchen garden or a large-scale commercial farm, I provide practical, evidence-based agronomic guidance tailored to your specific soil, climate, and crop requirements.
